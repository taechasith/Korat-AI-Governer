# Submission summary

This anonymous computational audit asks how a general-purpose AI agent changes a fictional 100-token Korat allocation when it receives frozen local public evidence and explicit epistemic constraints. Eleven official source snapshots became 12 jurisdiction-labelled evidence cards. A local TF-IDF RAG-Mock system retrieves up to eight cards, with complete logs. The frozen 8 scenario x 3 charter x 3 condition design records 72 sequential, non-independent trials.

Contributions: (1) a public-data-only reproducible audit pipeline; (2) a municipality/province-aware Korat evidence case; and (3) a design argument for exposing provenance, uncertainty, and normative charters rather than an apparent single AI answer.

Generated results: all 72 records meet allocation constraints; retrieval citation validity and factual traceability are 1.000 for observed cited claims; C2 uncertainty disclosure is 1.000 versus 0.000 in C1 eligible stress tests; C0-to-C1 mean JSD is 0.013, while mean charter sensitivity is 0.035.
