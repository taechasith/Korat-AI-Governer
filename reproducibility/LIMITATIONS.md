# Limitations and validity boundaries

1. The 72 cells are a single sequential within-agent audit, not independent samples or fresh-context replicates.
2. The corpus contains source/scope cards and planning evidence, not a complete municipal indicator system. Missing is null, not zero.
3. Province, PAO, national, and municipality evidence have different administrative scope. A citation is not a license to infer a city value.
4. Citation validity and traceability validate IDs and linkage, not full semantic entailment.
5. There is no human/expert baseline, no resident data, no actual fiscal budget, and no outcome evaluation.
6. Metrics cannot establish policy correctness, causal benefit from RAG, or readiness for municipal deployment.
7. Source pages, documents, and model behavior may change after the freeze date.
8. The PDF is compiled with the ACM review class but has not been validated against any particular venue's page limit or submission portal.
